# smith2017-malayic

Smith, Alexander D. 2017. *The languages of Borneo: a comprehensive classification*, Tables 59-60 (p. 184). A correction to batch `smith2017-borneo`.

## Why

By the rule the user set on 20 Nov (only safe information; correspondence tables and established microgroups, not intermediate reconstructions), the six rows that batch put on Proto-Malayic were taken out: Smith states no Proto-Malayic changes (p. 183), and the rows were only the reflexes all seven West Kalimantan lects share. The node stays (Malayic is an established group), with no changes of its own beyond the notation row (decision `pmal-shared`, updated).

The six rows were deleted from the sheet by hand and the Proto-Malayic note and decision updated there. This batch adds what the deletion leaves out: the same Table 59-60 rows (*-q > h, *-b > p, *-d > t, *-j- > d, *-j > t, *-əC > a) on each of the seven lects, as Smith prints them, and the retention rows that now become explicit.

## Scope

62 correspondence rows: 46 as printed (with the sporadic second reflexes of *-j-: n, r) and 16 stated retentions (Smith's tables list only changes, p. 50).

## Not entered

As in `smith2017-borneo`: the nasal before *-d-, *-j-, *-s- in some words (only the *-p-, *-t- ones are entered), Ketapang (rt) for *-d and *-j.

`data/batches/smith2017-borneo/` holds the whole Borneo batch as it now stands, these rows included.
