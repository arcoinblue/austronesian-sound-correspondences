# leewang1984-kosraean

Lee, Kee-dong & Judith W. Wang. 1984. Kosraean reflexes of Proto-Oceanic phonemes. In Bender (ed.), *Studies in Micronesian linguistics*, 403-442 (Pacific Linguistics C-80). Read: §1 (pp. 403-404), §2.8-2.9 (pp. 415-416), §3 (pp. 417-429), §4 (p. 432), note 4 (p. 436). Inventory from Lee (1975), already in the sheet.

## Scope

40 correspondence rows for Kosraean (`kosr1238`), all stated against PMc, and one decision.

- **Velarised and rounded consonants (23 rows, decision `ksr-secondary`).** Each is a reflex of the same PMc consonant as the plain one: pˠ (PMc *p), fˠ (*pʷ), mˠ (*m), kˠ kʷ (*k, *x), ŋˠ ŋʷ (*ŋ), tˠ tʷ (*t), sˠ sʷ (*T, *Z), ʂˠ ʂʷ (*c), lˠ lʷ (*r, *l), nˠ nʷ (*n). Environment `_[e,ɛ]` (Kosraean vowels: Lee & Wang find both series distinctive only there), status sporadic, since velarisation arose after PMc and is not predictable from the PMc form (p. 415). Marked composed: the PMc parent comes from the plain reflex.
- **Vowels (12 rows).** Non-final *i > i, *u > u, *o > o as the regular reflexes; the minor reflexes Lee & Wang list (ɨ, i, ɔ, ʌ) as sporadic with conditions unknown; *e > a from its one attested form; rounding assimilation (*i > u, *a > ɒ, *a > o before a round vowel in the next syllable) as sporadic.
- **Final vowel deletion (5 rows):** every PMc vowel > ∅ / _# (p. 429), with the note that inalienable nouns keep the stem vowel before a possessive suffix.

## Method

As the earlier Lee & Wang rows (decision `ksr-composition`): stated against POC in Grace's orthography, entered against PMc. The vowels take PMc's five vowels as POc's unchanged. Each row's notes keep the POC statement and the examples.

r was already changed to the approximant ɻ (with ɻˠ, ɻʷ) in the sheet, after note 4 (p. 436); the velarised and rounded series got their own columns in PR #8.

## Not entered

- *a has no regular reflex: it 'may be reflected as any of the twelve surface vowels' (p. 421). Only the rounded reflexes have a condition, and only those are entered.
- *a-raising before a high vowel (pp. 424-425): the resulting mid vowel is not predictable. High-vowel lowering before *a (pp. 425-426) and low-vowel dissimilation (pp. 427-428): Lee & Wang give almost as many counterexamples as examples, and call the dissimilation evidence 'scanty'.
- Monophthongisation (p. 427) and glide insertion (pp. 428-429).
- No rows for the ɻ series (loans and POC *(dr)), the glides j, jˠ, jʷ, w, wʷ, or a secondary series from PMc *s, whose regular reflex Lee & Wang and Bender et al. disagree on.
- The rows from Lee & Wang already in the sheet are still not marked `derivation` composed (decision `ksr-composition`).
